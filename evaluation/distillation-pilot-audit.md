Date: 2026-07-21

## Automated gates

- Provider: DeepSeek V4 Flash with isolated untrusted input, strict forced-function output, bounded JSON-object fallback, exact local schema validation, and a second redaction pass.
- Final refreshed cohort: 509 eligible agent-dialogue documents and 3,555 units; every document and unit is ready with zero pending or failed units.
- Every unit passed the exact eight-field schema, secret scan, raw ordinal-range check, stable-ID check, FTS parity, current model/prompt check, and 384-dimensional local-embedding check.
- SQLite passed `quick_check`, had zero foreign-key violations, and remained in WAL mode.
- The built-in 24-case distillation evaluation improved Recall@8 from 0.75 to 1.0 and MRR@8 from 0.570833 to 0.812897 (+42.4054%).
- The final fixed 24-query cross-source quality gate improved Recall@8 from 0.625 to 1.0 and MRR@8 from 0.472222 to 0.789931. Every top-eight set had distinct document IDs, every inferred scope was correct, and warm p95 was 986.252 ms.

## Groundedness remediation

Manual review found a model-generated claim that no assistant response existed even though the mapped raw evidence contained assistant messages. A local groundedness guard and mapped-evidence sanitizer were added. Sixteen initially affected units and seven chunk-boundary cases were selectively regenerated by stable input hash. The enabled-mode refresh exercised the guard on newly appended dialogue, and a final full-corpus rescan found zero remaining contradictions.
